## Challenge: [Find your age]

### Goal
The goal is to program the VR Robot to drive to your age as of today, program the VR Robot to drive to your age as of today, then have the VR Robot drive to your age in the year 2035, and Program the VR Robot to drive and mark your birthday.


Level 1: Program the VR Robot to drive to your age as of today. 

Level 2: Program the VR Robot to drive to your age as of today. Then have the VR Robot drive to your age in the year 2035.

Level 3: Program the VR Robot to drive and mark your birthday. 
Use a blue pen to mark what day you were born. 
Use a red pen to mark what month you were born.  
Use a green pen to mark what year you were born.
